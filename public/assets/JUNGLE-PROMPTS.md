## public/assets/jungle-wide.png

Use case: illustration-story. Create a wide 3:2 immersive full-bleed children's book jungle landscape for a website. Reference image is STYLE ONLY: match its richly textured colored-pencil and imperfect black felt-tip outlines on warm sand paper, warm expressive animals, absolutely no photorealism or 3D. Dense lush jungle framing all four edges: huge layered monstera leaves, ferns, vines, thick trees, foreground leaves and distant canopy. Palette sand #F7E7C6, leaf green #5BAE4A, forest #2F6B3D, mango #F28C28, sunshine #F6C445, river blue #5BC0EB. Leave a CLEAR SMALL CENTRAL SUNLIT GLADE at x35%-65%, y30%-62% for a web button overlay. Rich vegetation occupies most of image; not lots of empty sky. Integrate a large friendly elephant partly hidden in LEFT midground foliage, giraffe neck and head peeking high over RIGHT treetops, toucan on upper LEFT branch, two colorful parrots on RIGHT branches, friendly crocodile along blue river at LOWER RIGHT. Animals large and readable at different depths, partially hidden by leaves, belonging to landscape. Reserve upper left-middle at x22% for separate hanging monkey overlay, no monkey in this background. No cheetah (original logo will be overlaid in lower left foreground). No text, no signs, no typography, no frames, no UI. Highly detailed charming hand-drawn illustration, textured warm paper visible in every colored-pencil stroke.

## public/assets/jungle-monkey.png

Use case: illustration-story. Transparent PNG animation sprite sheet, exactly TWO identical-size panels side by side, image overall aspect ratio 3:2. Each half contains the SAME full body warm friendly little brown monkey dangling by one arm from a long curved leafy jungle vine starting at the very top of each panel. Tail curled, cute smile, other hand waving, entire monkey visible, feet near bottom. First panel eyes OPEN, second panel same monkey eyes CLOSED in a blink. Keep silhouette, pose, position and scale EXACTLY identical, only eyes change. Match supplied reference colored pencil on paper with irregular dark felt-tip outlines, orange-brown fur, cream face, leaf green #5BAE4A and forest #2F6B3D vine leaves. True transparent background, no scenery or shadow or text. Leave 10% horizontal margin inside each panel. This will be an independent animated layer over a children's book jungle.
